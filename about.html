Our Story

GA Goods Distribution is a London-based e-commerce distributor established in 2022. From the outset our goal has been simple: to build a distribution business grounded in long-term relationships, reliable supply and mutual value.

We work with a focused portfolio of trusted consumer brands, purchasing stock consistently and in volume. We operate across leading UK e-commerce marketplaces, covering consumer electronics, fragrances, beauty, DIY and FMCG. Stock is held at our own UK warehouse and fulfilment is supported by third-party logistics partners, enabling us to operate efficiently and scale without operational constraints.

One UK warehouse

East Sussex — inbound stock and onward distribution

Full transparency is at the core of how we operate. We believe honest and open communication with every supplier and partner is the only way to build something that lasts. Our suppliers always know where they stand with us.

Alongside our UK supply base we have established inbound supply routes for European and worldwide import. We buy directly from overseas brands and distributors, with the documentation and compliance processes in place to bring stock into the UK cleanly.

Since 2022 we have grown steadily and are striving for continued expansion in the coming years — broadening our brand portfolio, increasing our volume capacity and extending our reach across the UK and EU.

Our Ethos
